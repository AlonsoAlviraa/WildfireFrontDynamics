## 4. Objectives

1. Deploy **daily open wildfire intelligence packs** (FIRMS multi-sensor, STAC watch, honesty labels) for pilot regions.  
2. Provide **policy-based Fire Decision Cards** (GO/HOLD/ABSTAIN) with audit trail for training and preparedness.  
3. Define a safe pathway to fuse **operational thermal data** when end-users authorize (no open-only GO).  
4. Train practitioners; publish open methods and non-sensitive demos.  
5. Transfer lessons to UCPM Knowledge Network.

## 8. Ethics & limitations (mandatory text)

> The project will not issue tactical dispatch orders. Open satellite products are not official burned-area statistics. Personal data of evacuated populations will not be processed. Any operational data shared by authorities remains under their control and confidentiality rules.
