### A1) EFFIS — perímetros satélite (EN)

**Para:** `jrc-effis@ec.europa.eu`  
**Asunto:** Data request — burned area perimeters Spain (Castilla-La Mancha) 2024–2025 — independent R&D project

```
Dear EFFIS team,

I am Alonso Alvira. I develop an independent R&D project on fire-front
dynamics from airborne thermal (LWIR) sequences in Castilla-La Mancha
(Spain), working with regional emergency stakeholders (CMA / GEACAM).

What I build
------------
A pipeline that estimates rate of spread (ROS) from successive fire
fronts, sector-aware head/flank/rear speeds, and short-horizon advance
envelopes (15/30/60 min) for emergency decision support, with explicit
“blocked / low confidence” states when data are missing.

What I need
-----------
Independent burned-area or fire-perimeter vectors for geometric
validation (e.g. Hausdorff vs our aerial fronts) for selected fires in
Spain 2024–2025 — ideally near Tobarra / Albacete and other CLM events.

Could you advise how to obtain BA perimeters for those dates/areas, or
point me to the correct download / data request form?
I will also submit the online form on the EFFIS portal.

Project-internal / non-commercial use; no redistribution of restricted
products without permission.

Thank you,
Alonso Alvira
alonso.alvbal@gmail.com
```
